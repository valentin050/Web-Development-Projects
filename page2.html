What equipment do you need for a Triathlon?

In this page I will show you the information about the equipment needed for a triathlon!

Essential Equipment

Each discipline requires specific equipment:

  1. Swimming: swimsuit, goggles, swim cap, and possibly a wetsuit.
  2. Cycling: bicycle, helmet, cycling shoes, and glasses.
  3. Running: running shoes.

Additional Gear

Depending on the distance and weather, you may need extra gear for safety and comfort.

For example, a wetsuit can help in cold water, and a hydration system can be useful during longer races.

In longer races, you may choose to wear socks for cycling and running. In shorter races, some athletes go without them to save time during transitions.
